# PsychoPrompt 🧠

> **An AI system prompt that psychologically profiles you before any work begins — and adapts to your specific mind.**

Most productivity AI treats every person the same. PsychoPrompt doesn't.

Before helping you build your SaaS, launch your startup, or finish your project — it first understands *how your specific brain works*: your fears, your blockers, your motivation patterns, your avoidance triggers.

Then it works with you accordingly. Not against you.

---

## The problem it solves

You already know what to do. You have the idea. You have the tools. But something stops you — and it's different for every person:

- Some people fear judgment, so they never ship
- Some people start 10 projects and finish none
- Some people work only to satisfy external pressure and resist every step
- Some people think they're not smart enough when they just don't know yet
- Some people get paralyzed when they lose control of a process

Standard AI assistants ignore all of this. They give the same advice to everyone. PsychoPrompt maps your pattern first.

---

## How it works

1. **Copy the prompt** from `PROMPT.md`
2. **Paste it into the System Prompt field** of Claude, ChatGPT, or any AI assistant
3. **Start a conversation** — the AI will ask you 7 questions before anything else
4. **Receive your psychological profile** — your pattern, your blocker, and how the AI will adapt
5. **Work** — the AI now knows how to communicate with you specifically

That's it. No app. No account. No subscription. Just a prompt.

---

## The 6 psychological patterns it covers

| Pattern | Core Blocker |
|---|---|
| 🔭 The Paralyzed Visionary | Gap between ambition and current reality feels too large |
| ⛓️ The External Executor | Working for others, not yourself — brain refuses to comply |
| 🎯 The Perfectionist Avoider | Fear that imperfect work proves you're not enough |
| 👁️ The Fear-of-Judgment Avoider | One bad experience became a permanent rule |
| ⚡ The Chaotic Starter | Novelty addiction — excited to start, bored to finish |
| 🪞 The Capability Doubter | Confuses not-yet-knowing with being-incapable |

## Usage

### With Claude (recommended)
1. Go to [claude.ai](https://claude.ai)
2. Start a new project or conversation
3. Paste the contents of `PROMPT.md` as the system prompt
4. Begin your conversation normally

### With ChatGPT
1. Go to [chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com)
2. Create a custom GPT, or paste into a new conversation starting with "Use these instructions:"
3. Paste the prompt
4. Begin

### With any other AI
The prompt works with any instruction-following AI. Paste it at the start of your conversation.

## What the AI will never do after profiling you

- Tell you to "just do it" when your blocker is fear, not laziness
- Agree with "I'm not smart enough"
- Give you a list of 10 tasks when you're overwhelmed
- Let you plan endlessly without shipping anything
- Ignore avoidance patterns when they happen in real time

---

## Why this works

The prompt is based on patterns from cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT), motivational interviewing, and behavioral psychology — specifically applied to the context of building products and working on creative/technical projects.

The intake questions are designed to surface:
- The gap between external behavior and internal motivation
- The specific fear driving avoidance
- The person's relationship with failure and judgment
- Their executive function patterns
- Their genuine vs. imposed goals
